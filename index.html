BIRTHLY keeps the people you love, the dates that matter, and your thoughtful gift ideas together on your iPhone.

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Works offline

Start with someone special

Open People and tap +. Add their name, birthday and relationship. A birth year, photo and notes are optional.

Your Home shows upcoming birthdays and today’s celebrations. Browse the month in Calendar.

Open a person to add a gift idea, or use the Gifts tab. Save a budget and notes, then mark the gift as purchased when it is ready.

In Settings, enable birthday reminders and choose the timing that suits you.

Good to know

Why did a reminder not appear?

Enable Birthday reminders in BIRTHLY Settings, select at least one timing option, and check that reminders are enabled for the person. In iOS Settings, allow BIRTHLY notifications and check Focus and notification previews. Past reminder times are skipped. Reminders are scheduled for 9:00 AM. BIRTHLY queues the nearest 60 requests, so open the app regularly to refill the queue.

What happens when I travel?

Birthday dates stay the same. Open BIRTHLY after changing time zones so future reminders are recalculated for 9:00 AM in your current time zone.

How are February 29 birthdays handled?

BIRTHLY uses February 28 in non-leap years and returns to February 29 in leap years. You can save a February 29 birthday without entering a birth year.

Where are my photos and personal details stored?

Your data is stored locally in the app on your iPhone. BIRTHLY has no account or cloud sync. Photos are optional; the app stores a small copy of the image you select. Device backups follow your Apple settings.

How do I delete data?

Open a person or gift to delete it. Deleting a person also deletes their gift ideas and removes their scheduled reminders. Settings → Delete all people and gifts removes all saved app data. Deletion cannot be undone in BIRTHLY.

Can I change the appearance or text size?

Choose Dark, Light, or System in BIRTHLY Settings. Text follows your iPhone’s Dynamic Type settings, and the app supports VoiceOver.

Still need a hand?

Send a support request through the BIRTHLY support repository. A GitHub account is required. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem.

Support requests are public. Please do not include people’s birthdays, private notes, photos, phone numbers, or other personal information.
