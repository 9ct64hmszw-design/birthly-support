Privacy policy · November 11, 2026

Personal means
personal.

BIRTHLY is designed to keep your birthday reminders and gift plans on your iPhone.

Information stored in the app

You may enter names, birthdays, relationship labels, favorite status, photos, notes, gift ideas, budgets, currencies, and purchase status. BIRTHLY stores this information locally using Apple’s on-device storage. BIRTHLY does not transmit it to a developer-operated server, advertising service, analytics service, or external API.

No accounts, tracking, or advertising

BIRTHLY does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. The app does not collect personal information for the developer, track you across apps or websites, or sell personal information.

Optional photos

Apple’s photo picker lets you choose a photo. BIRTHLY receives only the image you choose, saves a reduced-size copy for the person, and does not retain the original image metadata. You can remove or replace that copy in the person’s editor.

Optional local notifications

When you enable reminders, BIRTHLY asks iOS for notification permission. Notifications are scheduled locally. They may display the saved person’s name on your lock screen; manage notification previews and permissions in iOS Settings. BIRTHLY does not use a remote push notification server.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Information remains in the app until you delete it. Delete a person to remove their details and related gift ideas. Use Settings → Delete all people and gifts to remove all saved app records. BIRTHLY does not provide cloud synchronization. Your device may back up app data according to your own Apple device and backup settings; Apple controls that backup process.

Support and this website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as an IP address, when serving the website under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Opening a website is separate from the app’s offline features.

If you choose to submit a support request on GitHub, the information you include is public and is processed by GitHub. Do not include birthdays, private notes, photos, or other personal information. The developer may use the details you voluntarily provide to respond to and resolve the request.

Children

The app does not collect information from users or include tracking or advertising. Its local storage and deletion controls apply to all users.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when BIRTHLY’s features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the BIRTHLY support channel. Keep requests general because this channel is public.
